# Chrome Web Store listing

## Store listing

**Summary** (132 chars max)
Reuse What's New and Promotional Text from previous versions in App Store Connect, for every language at once.

**Description**
Stop copy-pasting release notes language by language.

ASC Fill fills the version in preparation in App Store Connect with the texts of:
• the live version,
• or one of your last 10 versions.

Choose What's New, Promotional Text, or both. History builds itself: every version you copy or fill is kept.

Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking.

Open source: https://github.com/martinfrouin/asc-fill

**Category**: Developer Tools
**Language**: English

Graphics: `screenshot-1280x800.png`, `promo-440x280.png`, icon `icons/128.png`.

## Privacy practices

**Single purpose**
Fill the What's New and Promotional Text fields of an App Store Connect version with texts from a previous version.

**Permission justifications**
- `storage`: keeps the history of the last 10 versions' texts and the user's preferences, locally.
- `tabs`: reads the active tab's URL to know which app and platform are open, and loads the live version and the version in preparation in that tab.
- Host `https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/*`: reads and fills the version texts on App Store Connect pages.

**Remote code**: No.

**Data usage**: collects none of the listed data types. Texts are stored only on the user's device.
Check the three certifications (no selling, no unrelated use, no creditworthiness).
