# ASC Fill — Privacy Policy

ASC Fill collects no data.

- The texts it reads on App Store Connect (What's New, Promotional Text) and your preferences are stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`).
- Nothing is sent to any server. No analytics, no tracking, no account.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Contact: https://github.com/martinfrouin/asc-fill/issues
